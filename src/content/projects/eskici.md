## The problem

Eskici was our graduation project: a marketplace for second-hand goods. Buyers and sellers in Istanbul do not all share one language, so the interface had to work in Arabic, English and Turkish.

## My part

I led a team of three and wrote 90 of the project's 112 commits. I built the hardest parts myself: real-time messaging between buyers and sellers, and a reporting system for listings.

## Key decisions

- **Three languages from the start**, including a right-to-left layout for Arabic.
- **A reporting system** so that users could flag bad listings and the marketplace stayed trustworthy.
- **REST APIs on Express and MongoDB**, the stack the whole team could work in.

## The result

Eskici ran live for about a year. It is offline now; the code is on GitHub.
